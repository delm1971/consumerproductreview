How to choose the right gadget

Start with the problem, not the product

Write down what you want to fix: heat, a cramped screen, a dead battery. The best product is the one that solves that single problem well.

Check compatibility before you order

Ports, device models, room size and power needs decide whether a product works for you. Confirm them on the retailer’s page.

Read return and warranty terms

A fair return window protects you if a product is not a fit. Look for it before checkout.

Be careful with big claims

Treat health, performance and savings claims with care. Look for specifics from the maker and independent sources, and talk to a professional for health questions.
